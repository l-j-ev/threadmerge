How it works

Three steps, all inside Outlook.

No new inbox, no portal to forward into. Nootro lives in the compose window you already use.

01
Compose & control

Merge a thread, redact the rest

Pull two threads together or forward one. Strip out what stays private, and decide exactly what each recipient is allowed to see.

02
Send with a seal

Fingerprint, then send

Nootro takes a SHA-256 fingerprint of the message and embeds a verify badge before it ever leaves your outbox.

03
Recipients verify

Proof, on their side

Anyone who receives it can confirm on verify.nootro.ai that the content is authentic and nothing was changed in transit.

What's inside

Controlled sharing, with proof built in.

Controlled disclosure

Decide what each recipient can see and redact everything else. The thread leaves your outbox showing only what you chose.

Cryptographic verification

Every share carries a SHA-256 fingerprint your recipients can check independently. No login, no trust in us required.

Audit trail

Who shared what, with whom, and when. Every send is logged for your tenant and exports to CSV in a click.

Reusable templates

Save the redaction rules and share patterns your team uses again and again, so controlled sharing stays one click.

Hash-only storage

We keep a fingerprint of what you shared, never the email itself. Enough to prove authenticity, and nothing more.

BEC detection Coming

Flag business-email-compromise patterns before a forged thread spreads. On the roadmap and in design now.

What we store

A fingerprint, not your email.

  • Your message content never lands in our database. We store a one-way SHA-256 hash: a fingerprint that can confirm a match but can't be reversed back into the email.
  • Sign-in runs through your own Microsoft 365 tenant over Azure AD. Nootro acts on your behalf, never around you.
  • Recipients verify against the fingerprint alone. The verification page reveals authenticity, never the contents.
